Our Story

Started in a two-car garage, still run by the people who opened it.

Heliast began in 2011 when two electricians got tired of installing other companies' systems and watching corners get cut. They bought one used inverter, borrowed a pickup truck, and wired their first rooftop themselves.

That same hands-on standard still shapes how we work. Every Heliast crew member is a direct employee, certified in-house, on a system we designed ourselves — not a subcontractor working off someone else's blueprint.

Fifteen years later, we've kept the part that mattered: showing up, doing the wiring right, and answering the phone after the panels are on the roof.
